# 🎬 Video CV Script — Agrim Billowria

> **Target Duration:** 80 – 90 seconds  
> **Tone:** Confident, concise, professional yet personable

> **Total Word Count:** ~210 words (≈ 84 seconds at 150 wpm)

---

## 📌 Tips Before You Record

- Maintain eye contact with the camera (tape a small dot next to the lens).
- Speak at a calm, measured pace — rushing kills credibility.
- Pause briefly (1 second) between each section to let the viewer breathe.
- Dress sharp; solid dark colours work best on camera.
- Record in a clean, well-lit environment — natural light from the front is ideal.
